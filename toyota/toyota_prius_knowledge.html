Toyota Prius

Overview

The Toyota Prius is a hybrid passenger car known for fuel efficiency, aerodynamic design and Toyota's hybrid technology. The exact equipment, engine output, colours and specifications can vary by model year, grade and market.

Performance

  • Toyota Lanka lists a 1798cc gasoline hybrid 4-cylinder engine for its Prius page.
  • Toyota Lanka lists maximum torque of 185 Nm at 3,600 rpm.
  • Prius hybrid performance and output can vary by grade, model year and market.
  • Some newer Prius versions use a 2.0L hybrid system and can produce up to 196 hp; this is market/model-year dependent.
  • Toyota describes the Prius as having a low centre of gravity for stable handling.
  • Driving modes can include EV, Power, ECO and Normal, depending on grade and market.

Hybrid System

  • The Prius combines a gasoline engine with electric motor assistance.
  • The hybrid system can use electric power at suitable speeds and conditions.
  • Regenerative braking recovers energy during deceleration and braking.
  • The hybrid battery is automatically managed by the vehicle; drivers do not normally need to manually charge a standard non-plug-in Prius.
  • A Prius Plug-in Hybrid is a different vehicle configuration and should not be confused with the standard Prius.

Fuel Economy

Fuel economy varies significantly by model year, grade, drivetrain, driving conditions and market. For example, Toyota's U.S. 2026 Prius comparison data lists up to 57 mpg combined for a listed Prius configuration. Do not present this as a Sri Lankan specification unless the exact vehicle grade matches.

Exterior Design

  • Aerodynamic body design intended to reduce aerodynamic drag.
  • Slim LED headlights.
  • Distinctive rear light-bar styling.
  • Available lightweight 17-inch aluminium alloy wheels, depending on grade.
  • Rear liftgate/hatch design.

Interior & Comfort

  • Modern driver-focused interior design.
  • Digital/driver information display varies by grade and market.
  • Automatic air conditioning is available on current U.S. configurations.
  • Smart Key features are available on current U.S. configurations, depending on grade.
  • Power windows and power door locks are available on current U.S. configurations.

Technology & Connectivity

  • Multimedia display and connected services vary by model year and grade.
  • Navigation availability varies by configuration and market.
  • Bluetooth/phone connectivity and wireless smartphone features depend on the exact system and market.
  • Toyota App/connected services availability depends on vehicle, region and subscription.

Safety

  • Toyota Safety Sense features vary by model year and grade.
  • Current Prius documentation references Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 for applicable 2026 vehicles.
  • Available driver-assistance technologies can include Pre-Collision System, Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Departure Alert, Lane Tracing Assist, Road Sign Assist and Automatic High Beams, depending on configuration.
  • Integrated backup camera is listed by Toyota Lanka.
  • Driver-assistance systems are designed to assist the driver and do not replace attentive, responsible driving.

Colours

Toyota Lanka lists these Prius exterior colours as examples: Silver Metallic, Gray Metallic, Attitude Black, Emotional Red, Platinum White Pearl, Dark Blue and Mustard. Actual colours depend on grade, model year and local availability.

Warranty & Maintenance

  • Toyota U.S. states that hybrid batteries for Toyota hybrid vehicles from model year 2020 are covered for 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first, subject to warranty terms.
  • Warranty coverage differs by country and market. For AutoHub customers in Sri Lanka, do not promise U.S. warranty terms; confirm the local Toyota warranty.
