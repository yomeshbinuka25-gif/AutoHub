What is the Toyota Prius?

The Toyota Prius is a hybrid passenger car developed by Toyota. It combines a gasoline engine with electric motor assistance and is known for fuel efficiency and aerodynamic design. Exact specifications vary by model year, grade and market.

Is the Toyota Prius a hybrid car?

Yes. The Toyota Prius uses Toyota hybrid technology, combining a gasoline engine with electric motor assistance. A standard non-plug-in Prius does not normally require external charging because its hybrid battery is managed by the vehicle and can recover energy through regenerative braking.

What engine does the Toyota Prius have?

Engine size depends on model year and market. Toyota Lanka lists a 1798cc gasoline hybrid four-cylinder engine for its Prius page. Some newer Prius markets use a 2.0-litre hybrid system.

How many cylinders does the Toyota Prius engine have?

Toyota Lanka lists a four-cylinder gasoline hybrid engine for the Prius. Engine specifications can vary by model year and market.

What is the Toyota Prius engine capacity?

Toyota Lanka lists the Prius with a 1798cc engine. Some newer Prius versions in other markets use a 2.0-litre hybrid engine.

How much horsepower does the Toyota Prius have?

Horsepower depends on model year, grade and drivetrain. Some current Prius configurations can produce up to 196 horsepower. The exact output should be confirmed for the specific vehicle being sold.

What is the torque of the Toyota Prius?

Toyota Lanka lists maximum torque of 185 Nm at 3,600 rpm for its Prius specification.

What type of fuel does the Toyota Prius use?

The Toyota Prius uses gasoline together with a hybrid electric system. The exact fuel requirements should be confirmed for the relevant market and model year.

What is the Toyota Prius fuel tank capacity?

Toyota Lanka lists a 43-litre fuel tank for the Prius.

Is the Toyota Prius fuel efficient?

Yes. Fuel efficiency is one of the main design goals of the Prius. Exact fuel economy depends on model year, grade, drivetrain, driving conditions, traffic and driving style.

What is the fuel economy of the Toyota Prius?

Fuel economy varies by model year, grade and market. Current Toyota U.S. Prius configurations can achieve high fuel-economy figures, but a local AutoHub customer should be given the figure for the exact vehicle grade being sold.

Does the Toyota Prius need to be charged?

A standard non-plug-in Toyota Prius does not normally need to be connected to an external charger. Its hybrid battery is charged by the hybrid system and regenerative braking. Plug-in Hybrid versions are different and can be externally charged.

How does the Toyota Prius hybrid system work?

The Prius combines a gasoline engine with electric motor assistance. The system automatically manages engine and electric power according to driving conditions, while regenerative braking can recover energy and send it back to the hybrid battery.

What is regenerative braking in the Toyota Prius?

Regenerative braking recovers some energy when the vehicle slows down. The hybrid system converts part of that energy into electrical energy and stores it in the hybrid battery.

What driving modes does the Toyota Prius have?

Depending on the model year and grade, Prius driving modes can include EV, ECO, Normal and Power. Available modes vary by market and configuration.

What are the main features of the Toyota Prius?

Common Prius features can include hybrid powertrain technology, aerodynamic styling, LED lighting, modern interior technology, multimedia connectivity, driver-assistance systems and regenerative braking. Exact equipment varies by model year and grade.

What safety features does the Toyota Prius have?

Depending on model year and grade, Prius safety technology can include Toyota Safety Sense features such as Pre-Collision System, Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Departure Alert, Lane Tracing Assist, Road Sign Assist and Automatic High Beams. Exact equipment varies by market and grade.

Does the Toyota Prius have Toyota Safety Sense?

Applicable Prius models include Toyota Safety Sense features. The exact Toyota Safety Sense version and available functions depend on model year, grade and market.

What is the Toyota Prius transmission?

The Prius uses Toyota's hybrid drive system and an electronically controlled continuously variable transmission or e-CVT-type arrangement depending on model and market.

The Prius can be well suited to city driving because its hybrid system can efficiently manage low-speed driving and frequent stops. Actual fuel economy depends on traffic, driving style and vehicle condition.

What is the difference between a Prius and a Prius Plug-in Hybrid?

A standard Prius is a conventional hybrid whose battery is charged by the vehicle's hybrid system and regenerative braking. A Prius Plug-in Hybrid has a larger battery and can also be charged externally.
